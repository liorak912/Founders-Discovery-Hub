---
name: Discovery product boundaries
description: Product intent, evidence constraints, and initial persistence decision.
---
Discovery is for first-time founders doing customer discovery, not sales prospecting.
**Why:** The user requires hypothesized pains to be labeled, no invented statistics, no private-individual listings, and interviews grounded in past behavior.
**How to apply:** Keep generated research separate from actual interview evidence, preserve uncertainty in synthesis, and only use verbatim supplied notes for quotes. Outreach remains an editable template the founder sends themselves.

Initial projects are saved in the user's browser, with explicit disclosure, rather than a shared anonymous server database.
**Why:** No accounts were requested; browser-local storage avoids exposing founders' notes across anonymous visitors. Cross-device persistence requires a deliberate authenticated ownership design.
**How to apply:** Do not imply cloud backup or cross-device sync. Preserve drafts, notes, outreach edits and conversations together with each project.
